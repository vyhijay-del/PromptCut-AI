How PromptCut works

1. Add an audio track and your video clips.

2. Choose Voice + Music or Music Only, then describe the visual style.

3. PromptCut measures audio energy, estimates beat-like peaks and creates a master prompt plus timed scene instructions.

Prototype note: Browser-side beat detection is an estimate, not a studio-grade beat tracker. This version generates the edit plan; it renders a WebM video with selectable transitions, camera movement and rotation. It does not understand clip contents with a vision model.
